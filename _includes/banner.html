About Me

I like problems that don't have a clean answer yet, and I've spent my career on the "figure it out first" end of data science and applied machine learning. I wrote the charge/discharge logic for a fleet of grid-scale Li-ion batteries. I created the algorithm that tells a wearable device when to fire e-stim to augment a muscle contraction. I helped teams evaluate the behavior and efficacy of their LLM systems. I ran a boutique data science/ML consultancy that grew to ten engineers with seven figure revenue; growing that team taught me I like building people as much as building systems. This trajectory began with a master's in structural engineering from Stanford University, where I first internalized that data, models, and algorithms can leave a mark on the world.

Outside of work, I'm generally moving. I have a running habit that I've been pursuing relentlessly for a decade. I love every distance from 1 mile to 100 miles. I've been cycling and skiing nearly as long as I've been able to walk, and I'm discovering how hard it is to learn to surf as an adult. When I'm not outside, I like to tinker. I think about the elements of cooking that result in a great meal. I crochet stuffed animals. I had a backyard woodworking phase where I built a dozen pieces of furniture. I fret over the state of my houseplants as I try to strike the perfect environmental conditions.
