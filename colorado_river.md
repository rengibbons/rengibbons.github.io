<p>The Colorado River is promised out faster than it delivers, on a snowpack
that's shrinking, melting earlier, and turning into less runoff than it used
to. This is that story in the river's own data -- refreshed monthly from live
government sources (USGS, Bureau of Reclamation, and NRCS SNOTEL).</p>
